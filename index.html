About Me

From Quality Assurance to Product Strategy

I've spent over 5 years on the front lines of product delivery — leading QA teams, managing sprint cycles, coordinating cross-functional releases, and validating AI/LLM outputs. This hands-on experience taught me something valuable: the best products are built when someone deeply understands both the user's needs and the engineering reality.

That realization drove me to formalize my PM skills through Airtribe's AI-First Product Management program, where I completed a comprehensive capstone on Paytm, conducted 30+ user interviews, built interactive prototypes, and scored 30/30 on 5 out of 6 evaluated projects.

Today, I bring a rare combination: the rigor of a quality engineer, the strategic thinking of a product manager, and real experience shipping products across banking, AI systems, IoT, and web platforms.
